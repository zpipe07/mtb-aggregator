45NRTH Gravdal TR Studded 700c Tires - Width: 38mm, 45mm

Versatile and confidence inspiring, the Gravdal securely navigates through harsh winter conditions with concave carbide studs far added traction in icy conditions

  • Siping creates a more conforming lug for added traction
  • Concave carbide studs provide traction in the iciest conditions
  • Reflective strip on sidewall makes you more visible when riding in low-light conditions
  • Fits most bikes with cantilever or disc brakes
  • Tubeless ready
  • Concave carbide aluminum studs
  • Tire Diameter: 700c
  • ISO Diameter: 622 / road / 700c
  • Labeled Size: choose; 700 x 38c, or 700 x 43c
  • Tire Intended Use: Winter-Studded
  • Tire Type: Tubeless Ready Clincher
  • PSI: 65
  • TPI: 60
  • Tire Bead: Folding
  • Weight: 841g. (700x43c)
  • Color: Black
