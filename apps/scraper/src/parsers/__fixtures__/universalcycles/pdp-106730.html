Odyssey Thunderbolt Crankset

The Thunderbolt+ is Odyssey's tried and true Thunderbolt cranks, but now features updated graphics. The Thunderbolt+ is one of the most technically advanced crank designs on the market. Due to continued advancements in Odyssey's 41-Thermal processing and heat-treatment system, fatigue life has been increased by a massive margin

  • Proven 41-Thermal processed 4130 chromoly
  • Laboratory tested and team proven
  • Traditional sprocket interface
  • Updated open-ended spindle cap
  • New wedge cluster band with greater flexibility
  • Never-Wobble crank arm and spindle interface guarantee
  • Bottom bracket sold separately
  • Bore: 22
  • Crank Length: 175mm
  • Spindle Material: Steel
  • Crankset Style: 3-Piece
  • Drive Side Compatibility: Right Hand Drive
  • Crank Arm Material: Steel
  • BB Style: Mid
  • BB Included: No
  • Color: Rust Proof Black
