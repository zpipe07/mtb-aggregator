Cane Creek eeSilk Alloy Gravel Suspension Seatpost

Ride longer, ride farther with the eeSilk premium suspension seatpost from Cane Creek

  • Comparable in weight to traditional rigid performance seatposts while offering 20mm of vertical compliance
  • Reduces fatigue on long rides and improves control on rough surfaces or gravel
  • Pedal to saddle distance remains nearly constant through travel
  • #5 elastomer comes pre-installed, other weight elastomers sold separately
  • Post: forged aluminum w/ machined finish
  • Linkage: CNC machined aluminum
  • Pivots: Hard anodized aluminum axles IGUS Bushings/titanium bolts
  • Di2 battery compatible
  • Diameter: 31.6mm
  • Length: 375mm
  • Travel: 20mm
  • Max Rider Weight: 330lbs
  • Offset: 8mm
  • Weight: 350g. (31.6)
  • Color: black
