Fox Transfer Dropper Seatposts - Performance Elite Series, 2021/2023

The proven Transfer seatpost gets lighter and tighter for 2021. Completely redesigned from the ground up, with improved serviceability, decreased weight, reduced length, and decreased insertion depth

  • Performance Elite Transfer Posts retain the same internals, diameter options, drop options, shortened stack heights, new patent-pending head design and proven internals as the Factory Series but have a black anodized upper
  • Patent-pending clamp design dramatically reduces stack height
  • Proven Transfer internals with even easier serviceability than 2020 model
  • 25g lighter than 2020 Transfer
  • NEW lightweight under-bar 1x lever design – or existing 2x lever (remote sold separately)
  • Updated design
  • Low lever force provides consistent actuation pressure even with saddle weighted or unweighted
  • Cable actuated with tool-free quick disconnect for easier installation and removal
  • Available with Internal Cable routing only
  • Available in 30.9 or 31.6 diameter
  • Drop options: 100mm, 125mm, 150mm, 175mm, or 200mm

  • 100mm: 308.6mm total length; 170.3mm max insertion
  • 125mm: 363.5mm total length; 200.2 max insertion
  • 150mm: 418.3mm total length; 230mm max insertion
  • 175mm: 475.1mm total length; 261.9mm max insertion
  • 200mm: 530.7mm total length; 291.8mm max insertion
  • Minimum Insertion: 100mm from bottom of post on 100-175mm; 150mm from bottom of post on 200
  • Color: Black

  • NOTE: Remote Not Included. Fox Racing Shox Remote Lever Sold Separately.

  • Tech Spec Drawing
