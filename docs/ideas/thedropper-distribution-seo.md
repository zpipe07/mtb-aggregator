# The Dropper — distribution, SEO, and business viability

## Problem Statement

How might we grow The Dropper into a durable product by capturing **buyer-intent traffic** (especially SEO), **earning repeat usage**, and operating as a **legitimate trade or business**—without thinning the site into low-value programmatic pages?

## Recommended Direction

Treat growth as **distribution × differentiation**. Long-term discovery should lean on **search and email**: indexable hubs that match how people shop (category, brand, price bands, a few headline specs), expanded **only where Search Console demand and deal inventory justify it**. UX filters can stay richer than indexed URL sets; canonical and indexing rules should prevent infinite low-value URLs.

Monetization should assume **affiliate-first** with clean outbound tracking and program compliance—not optimistic “visitor → purchase” math before measurement. Display ads are a later layer; **email** is the main non-Google compounding channel once there is a clear value prop on each send.

On the business side: **separate finances, track expenses, and show profit intent** (execution + light revenue) rather than front-loading entity choice. LLC or other structure is a **liability/branding/CPA** decision, not a prerequisite to “make taxes work.”

## Key Assumptions to Validate

- [ ] **High-intent SEO can reach meaningful affiliate clicks** — Use Google Search Console (queries, pages, CTR) plus affiliate dashboards; define a minimum bar before adding new programmatic clusters.
- [ ] **Deal inventory stays dense enough on indexable hubs** — e.g. treat ~5 listings as a floor for “indexable landing page”; prefer ~10+ for stronger pages; noindex or merge thin clusters.
- [ ] **Aggregation + filtering + freshness** is differentiated enough versus retailer mailers and forums — Qualitative feedback + return visits; optional alerts/watchlists once reliability is acceptable.
- [ ] **Affiliate and retailer rules** allow the intended titles, pricing display, and link patterns — Audit major programs before scaling URL/templates.
- [ ] **Structured markup** (if any) improves visibility without accuracy risk — Prefer conservative schema (e.g. organization/site) until product/offer markup matches reality and policies.

## MVP Scope

**In**

- Technical SEO baseline: stable canonical host, sitemap/robots, metadata, performance (existing direction in the web app).
- **Curated indexable set**: category hubs, high-value brand+category and category+price pages **gated by minimum deal count**; internal links from parent hubs to children.
- Short, non-thin module on money pages: 2–4 sentence intro + optional light FAQ where it helps; refresh implied by real deal churn.
- Affiliate plumbing: disclosure, compliant links, click tracking, first merchant relationships—even small revenue validates the model.
- Bookkeeping habit: dedicated account (or clear separation), categorized spend/income.

**Out (for MVP)**

- Full alerts/watch price product (until core listing quality and email narrative are stable).
- “Every filter combination” indexation.
- Scaling display ads before traffic and RPM justify the UX cost.
- Relying on community channels (Reddit/forums) as the primary acquisition engine—they stay **experimental**, not core.

## Not Doing (and Why)

- **Blind programmatic explosion** — Creates thin crawl waste and helpful-content risk; expansion should follow **intent + inventory + GSC** evidence.
- **Over-precise long-tail URL matrices** (e.g. deep spec stacks) without search demand or stock — Engineering-friendly, SEO-weak; merge into filters or richer hubs instead.
- **Aggressive Product/Offer structured data** sitewide without merchant accuracy — Downside outweighs upside for aggregators if wrong or misleading.
- **Entity/tax structuring as a substitute for product traction** — Doesn’t replace distribution or revenue path; CPA when numbers warrant it.

## Open Questions

- Which **affiliate programs** and retailer rules constrain titles, snippets, and price presentation for The Dropper’s templates?
- What **minimum listing count and freshness** thresholds should hard-gate indexing per cluster (by category maturity)?
- When to invest in **slug-based deal URLs** vs numeric IDs for shareability and long-tail snippets?
- What **single primary differentiation** headline should every new visitor grasp in under one scroll (beyond “another deal list”)?
- At what revenue or liability threshold does **formal entity choice** merit a CPA conversation?
